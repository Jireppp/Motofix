Delete Your FXON Account and Data

App: FXON (package com.fikri.cekmotor) · Developer: Fikri Anugrah

You can delete your FXON account and all associated data at any time, either inside the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open FXON and go to the Help screen.
  2. Scroll to Identity & Data Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete Account & All Data (or Wipe Guest Garage Data if you are using a guest session) and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email from the address linked to your FXON (Google) account. Guest sessions have no email address and can only be deleted inside the app.

Email a deletion request

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and user ID)
  • All registered motorcycles (name, brand, plate number, odometer)
  • All tracked spare parts and service intervals
  • All maintenance logs and odometer logs

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Routine encrypted database backups that may contain your data are overwritten automatically within 30 days.
