You can delete your FXON account and all associated data at any time, either inside the app or by sending us a request. Choose the option that suits you.

01Delete inside the app

Option 1 · Instant

Use the Help screen

  1. Open FXON and go to the Help screen.
  2. Scroll to Identity & Data Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete Account & All Data (or Wipe Guest Garage Data if you are using a guest session) and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

02Request deletion by email

Option 2 · Within 30 days

Can’t open the app?

Send an email from the address linked to your FXON (Google) account. Guest sessions have no email address and can only be deleted inside the app.

Email a deletion request

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

03What is deleted and what is kept

DataStatus
Your account (email address and user ID)Deleted
All registered motorcycles (name, brand, plate number, odometer)Deleted
All tracked spare parts and service intervalsDeleted
All maintenance logs and odometer logsDeleted

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Routine encrypted database backups that may contain your data are overwritten automatically within 30 days.
