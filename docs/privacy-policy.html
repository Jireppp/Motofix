FXON (“the app”) is a motorcycle maintenance tracker. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

01Data we collect

  • Account data: if you sign in with Google, your email address and a unique user ID. If you use the app as a guest, an anonymous user ID is created instead.
  • App data you enter: motorcycle name and brand, plate number, odometer readings, tracked spare parts and service intervals, and maintenance logs (replacement date, kilometer reading, brand name and cost).
  • Local device data: preferences such as the reminder setting and active vehicle, stored on your device.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and it does not show ads.

02How we use your data

  • To provide the service: store your vehicles, calculate remaining service distance, and show your service history.
  • To sync your data to your account so it is not lost when you change devices.
  • To send a local reminder notification when you have not updated your odometer for 7 days. These notifications are scheduled on your device and can be turned off in the app.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

03Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts the database and authentication. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Google Sign-In is used for optional account sign-in and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps: the “Locate Nearby Mechanics” button opens Google Maps in an external app or browser. FXON does not read your location.

04Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (vehicles, tracked parts, service logs and odometer logs) at any time inside the app: Help screen → Identity & Data Privacy → Delete Account & All Data. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion on our account deletion page or by emailing us at the address below from the email linked to your account.

05Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted per user with row-level security rules on the database.

06Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page.
