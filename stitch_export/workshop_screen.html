Maintenance Playbook
ENGINE & FLUIDS
Engine Oil
Change oil every 2,000 - 4,000 km depending on oil grade and riding severity. Depleted oil accelerates piston ring wear.
#VITAL #ROUTINE
Radiator Coolant
Inspect coolant level every 8,000 km. Depleted coolant leads to sudden engine overheating and head gasket deformation.
#LIQUID
Air Filter
Clean at every routine check; replace when clogged (typically around 15,000 km). Ensures stoichiometric air-fuel combustion.
#FILTER
BRAKING SYSTEM
Brake Pads
Inspect pad lining monthly. Replace immediately if squeaking or metallic noise occurs to save disc rotors.
#SAFETY
