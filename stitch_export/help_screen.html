OPERATING MANUAL

MOTOFIX TRACKER // REV.01
I. WORKFLOW
1. REGISTER VEHICLE
Add your motorcycle in the GARAGE tab by entering the model name and registration plate number.
2. TRACK SPARE PARTS
Open the vehicle dashboard and select which components to track with custom service intervals.
3. UPDATE ODOMETER
Log your current odometer reading regularly so remaining distance forecasts stay accurate.
4. LOG REPLACEMENTS
Whenever a part is serviced or swapped, record a replacement log to reset its service cycle.
II. NOTICES & REMINDERS
Odometer Sync Required
Before executing a LOG REPLACEMENT action, ensure the global vehicle ODOMETER reflects the current physical dashboard reading. Failure to sync will result in skewed projection intervals.
Threshold Rules
System flags shift to WARNING status when threshold approaches ≤ 100km. OVERDUE status is triggered at sub-zero ranges. Logbook persists all historical actions securely.
Weekly Odometer Reminder (ACTIVE)
Dispatches a local reminder notification if your motorcycle odometer has not been updated for 7 consecutive days.
III. IDENTITY & DATA PRIVACY
The system is currently operating in local persistence mode (GUEST). To synchronize telemetry across fleet terminals and prevent data volatility, bind a permanent Google credential.
Account & Data Deletion: Permanently delete your account, all registered motorcycles, and maintenance history.
